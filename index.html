            Welcome to Olimjon Qudratov's portfolio

            This site shows the work of Olimjon Qudratov, a student from Uzbekistan who builds robots and websites. Here's how to find your way around.

            The globe

            The 3D Earth in the middle is the heart of the site. Drag it to spin it. The countries glowing in teal are places where Olimjon has competed and won. Click one to see his achievements there.

            The tabs on the left

            • Search (magnifying glass) finds anything on the site. Just start typing.
            • Achievements (trophy) lists his robotics results by country, including 1st place in Uzbekistan at WRO 2025 and a spot at the Singapore finals.
            • Projects (wrench) shows what he's building, like an AI-powered conveyor belt that's coming soon.
            • Websites (globe icon) collects sites he made, such as Tarjimon, a translator into the new Uzbek alphabet, and Dot Sheet, which turns your words into black-and-white dot art. Each one has screenshots and a "Visit site" button.
            • Certificates (badge) has his IELTS results, with the scores laid out in a table.
            • Contact (envelope) has his email, Telegram, LinkedIn, GitHub and X.
            • Ask AI (chat bubble) lets you ask a question and get an answer right away. You can ask about Olimjon or about anything else.

            Little extras

            Move your mouse across the sky and the stars react. Your browser's Back button takes you to the tab you were on before. The site works on phones too.
